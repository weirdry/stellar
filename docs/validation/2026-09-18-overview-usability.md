They are observations, not a CI timing threshold or phone-device benchmark.

base revision's roughly 20–24 ms; desktop timings are broadly unchanged.
Do not treat these numbers as a frame-time guarantee for all cameras, fonts or devices.

## Bounded behavior

Fit preserves distinguishable overview area/group dots and focused issue targets
rather than forcing every node into a short stage. Some dense or short maps require pan, minimap, tree selection or manual
zoom; names with no clear placement remain hidden. Automatic fit starts at an
area if its readable crop would otherwise have no area name. Manual pan/zoom can
still move content behind controls. Full identities remain in accessible names,
the tree, tooltip and inspector.

Curve candidates are finite. Remaining dense-graph crossings and large detours
are possible, and no universally obstacle-free routing claim is made. The
[viewer policy](../../assets/viewer/README.md) owns the current placement rules.
